what calendar alarm reads

Calendar Alarm examines page-created alerts, page-created Web Notifications (including those shown through the page's service worker registration), and reminder dialogs displayed by calendar.google.com. Unrelated alert/dialog text is discarded immediately. Because Google Calendar uses page-created Web Notifications as its desktop reminder channel and their titles may contain only an event name, every page-created Web Notification on calendar.google.com is treated as a reminder signal.

It also reads whether Chrome notifications are allowed for calendar.google.com, to show setup status in the popup.

what stays on your device

  • The most recent reminder text and local diagnostic timestamps.
  • Reminder text temporarily stored while a snooze is pending.
  • The Calendar notification permission state and the ID of the Calendar tab the extension opened.
  • Your enabled, alarm-window, sound, and keep-Calendar-open preferences. Chrome may sync these four settings through your Chrome profile.

what is never sent to us

Calendar Alarm does not transmit reminder text or browsing activity to the developer or any third party. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, account system, or developer-operated server. It never uses your Google credentials or the Google Calendar API. User data is not sold.

retention and deletion

A pending snooze is deleted after it fires. Other local data remains until replaced, cleared through Chrome, or removed when you uninstall the extension.

permissions

  • alarms schedules snoozes and a once-a-minute check that a Calendar tab is open.
  • notifications shows persistent reminders.
  • storage saves preferences and local reminder state.
  • calendar.google.com access is limited to detecting reminders on that site and, when "keep Calendar open" is enabled, finding, opening, or keeping awake a Calendar tab. No other sites are accessed.

limited use

Calendar Alarm's use of information received from Google services adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only for the extension's disclosed reminder-amplification feature.
